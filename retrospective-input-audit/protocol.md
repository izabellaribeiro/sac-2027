# Procedure and limitations

## Objective and unit

Assess whether each displayed textual change supports its intended concern and identify qualifications before interpreting agent responses. The unit is a baseline–variant pair, not an isolated question or a complete project backlog.

## Selection

Selection was purposive and iterative. Six initial cases were used to examine actor omission, allegedly missing action, purported contradiction, vague conditions and unavailable context. A further 18 cases were fixed using project labels and transformation types to create a 24-pair set. At the author's request, twelve pairs from twelve additional parent stories were selected, four per profile. The final set includes 12 pairs per profile and 12 pairs per project label. New selections were made from input texts without consulting their outputs during selection, but earlier exposure to selected outputs prevents a fully blinded-selection claim. No randomization, statistical representativeness, saturation or power claim is made.

## Preparation and provenance

The initial interpretations were developed by the first author. A second author reviewed the assessments, and they reached a consensus on all 36 pairs.

## Human review

The review presented the exact baseline and variant, or the exact insertion/deletion with the rest explicitly unchanged, alongside a proposed qualified interpretation in Portuguese. The author could agree or correct individual cases and explicitly agreed with all displayed judgments, in rounds of 3, 3, 6, 6, 6 and 12 cases. The final 12 were visually split into two groups of six and confirmed together. English judgments in the casebook are translations/paraphrases of the confirmed interpretations, not new independent ratings.

The review distinguished:

1. Whether the textual change is present.
2. Whether the intended concern is supported, as opposed to merely suggested by its construction label.
3. Whether related uncertainty already exists in the baseline.
4. Whether other changes or concerns were introduced simultaneously.
5. Whether sufficient context exists to judge a formal QUS violation.

For example, removing an actor phrase establishes explicit omission but not inability to infer a user. Replacing I want with malformed wording does not establish action absence if the action remains. Referencing an unavailable policy establishes a context need, not automatically a Complete violation. An unidentified predecessor establishes an unspecified dependency, not necessarily an unnecessary dependency. These distinctions were applied through case-specific judgments rather than a scored validation instrument.

## Status of judgments

After Author-A's initial review, Author-B reviewed the 36 judgments. As reported by Author-A, Author-B initially agreed with 28 and disagreed with eight (cases 17, 18, 22, 24, 31, 32, 35 and 36). A subsequent meeting resolved these disagreements; the author explicitly confirmed that the existing judgments were retained in all eight. Thus all 36 final judgments remain unchanged. The original alternative judgments, reasons for disagreement and meeting date were not supplied and are not reconstructed.

This is retrospective two-coauthor review with discussion-to-consensus. Independence and blinding of the initial judgments are not established. The reported 28/36 initial agreement is descriptive endorsement agreement, not an estimate of independent inter-rater reliability. No kappa is computed. Confirmation applies only to displayed cases and propositions; no unseen cases were validated by analogy. Judgments may contain multiple concerns and do not establish single-defect isolation. This add-on does not certify baselines as defect-free or establish all 13 QUS criteria.

## Separation from response analysis

No response-coverage results are included. Future analysis should preserve these input judgments and assess baseline and variant responses separately. Cases with insufficient evidence for a formal QUS defect must not be silently converted into validated defects. If analyzing a restricted subset, report the eligibility rule and denominator and retain excluded cases and reasons. Do not choose eligibility according to model performance. A question mentioning changed vocabulary is not automatically evidence that it addresses the intended structural concern.

## Existing data preserved

No model executions were performed. No original story, response, construction label or 100-question coding record was overwritten. No publication of this add-on is implied by its local generation.
